# business-storytelling

Use it when you have to persuade people, not just inform them: a pitch, a keynote, a proposal for management, a customer case study, or numbers that have to stick. It chooses a proven story structure for your audience and goal, and builds your content into it.

It doesn't activate for neutral informative texts such as reports and minutes (use [plain-writing](../plain-writing/README.md) for those), marketing page copy, ads, cold emails, social media posts or fiction.

## What it does

- It asks what is missing about the format, the audience, the action you want, where the audience stands now and how much time you have. If nobody can answer, it states its assumptions after the result.
- It chooses one framework and says why: Monroe's Motivated Sequence to drive action, Duarte's Sparkline for change, SCQA with the answer first for executives, or the Hero's Journey for case studies. The SUCCESs checklist runs on top of every story.
- It makes the story concrete with stakes, conflict, names and numbers, and opens with a hook instead of background.
- It turns data into an insight with context, a human scale and a comparison.
- It never invents facts. Missing numbers, customers or quotes become visible placeholders, listed after the result.
- It writes in the language of your audience.

## Before and after

> Acme was founded in 2009 and has grown into a leading provider of logistics software. Our platform offers route planning, fleet tracking and reporting. Last year we onboarded a new customer, a logistics company, which saw improvements after implementation.

> A 50-person logistics firm in Rotterdam lost two drivers a month to overtime. Six months after switching to route planning, overtime is down 40% and nobody has quit. *(Hero's Journey; the numbers came from the customer interview.)*

## Opinions you may not share

This skill takes positions. Change them in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) if they don't suit you.

- Never open with company history or credentials.
- For executives, the answer comes first.
- The customer is the hero of a case study, not your company.
- One framework per story, with SUCCESs only as a checklist.

## Works well with

- [plain-writing](../plain-writing/README.md) for informative texts around the story, such as a memo with the background.
- [ia-presenter](../ia-presenter/README.md) to turn the story into an iA Presenter file.
