# English

Use this file when the text is in English. The general rules in SKILL.md still apply.

## Form of address

Use *you* and *your*.

## Words to cut

- Empty intensifiers: *actually, various, certain, particular, basically, essentially, quite, really, very* and *just*.
- Redundant pairs: *first and foremost, full and complete, each and every, end result, past history* and *future plans*.
- Wordy phrases: *due to the fact that* becomes *because*, *in order to* becomes *to*, *at this point in time* becomes *now*, *a large number of* becomes *many*, *in the event that* becomes *if* and *has the ability to* becomes *can*.
- Slow openings: *It is important to note that*, *What this means is that*, *In this article we will*, *It should be noted that* and *Needless to say*.

## Transition words

Use plain transition words: *because, so, but, first, then, still* and *that's why*.

Avoid formal transition words: *moreover, furthermore, additionally, hence, thus, consequently* and *nevertheless*.

## Vague words

Avoid *aspect, process, factor, solution, framework* and *approach* when they carry no concrete meaning.

## Banned words

Never use: *delve, tapestry, realm, landscape, navigate* (figurative), *unlock, game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, groundbreaking, seamless, leverage, utilize, facilitate, pivotal, intricate, comprehensive, In today's fast-paced world* and *It's important to note*.

## Sentence structure and word choice

- Avoid nominalisations, especially nouns ending in *-tion, -ment* and *-ance* combined with verbs such as *make, conduct, perform* and *carry out*. Write *decide* instead of *make a decision*.
- Put the main clause first. Add conditions and subordinate clauses after it.
- Use plain words: *use* instead of *utilize*, *help* instead of *facilitate*, *start* instead of *commence*, *end* instead of *terminate* and *buy* instead of *purchase*.
- Describe people inclusively and as they describe themselves. For example, write *a person with autism*, unless the person prefers otherwise.
