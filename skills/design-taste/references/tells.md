# Defaults: the dated registry

A default is a device a model produces without a reason. Most began as good ideas on good work. They became defaults by being produced everywhere, for every subject. Using one is not wrong when the brief or the direction asks for it. Reaching for one when the choice was free means you were not deciding.

This list is dated because defaults change. Every published "use this instead" list has become the next wave of defaults within months. That is why no entry names a replacement font, colour or product. Each names a procedure.

**Basis** says why an entry is here:
- **evidence:** more common on weak work than on strong work in Taste Skill's study of 247 gallery sites, scored by model reviewers (top = 7.5 or higher, weak = under 6.5). The figures are top against weak.
- **convergence:** both studied skills name it as a generated default.
- **house:** this skill's opinion. You may disagree.

Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Pages and screens: first wave (2023–2024)

| Default | Basis | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Violet or indigo gradients on hero, buttons and glows | evidence: gradient fills 22% against 52% | Flat colour from the committed palette |
| Gradient text | evidence: 2% against 13% | Solid ink; emphasis from size or weight |
| Soft shadow under every card, glows | evidence: shadows on resting elements 24% against 48% | A tone step between surfaces; shadows only on what floats, such as a menu or dialog |
| Three identical cards with an icon in a rounded square | evidence: icon-topped feature cards 1% against 28% | A section composed for its content |
| Emoji as icons or bullets | evidence: 10% against 26% | Words, a drawn icon set, or a real picture |
| Centred headline, subtitle, two buttons, screenshot below | evidence: centred statement with media below 7% against 24% of hero types | A first view composed from the direction |
| Inter, or the system face, as the voice of an own-world page | convergence | A face chosen from the subject's world, with a reason |

| Default | Basis | Instead |

| Pill badge above the headline ("New", "Beta") | evidence: 14% against 34% | Say it in the supporting sentence |

## Pages and screens: third wave (2025–2026), the fix that became the look

The published remedy for the first two waves was "make it warm and editorial". Models adopted it wholesale.

| Default | Basis | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream or ivory ground with a terracotta, rust or clay accent | convergence; one site in 246 had a terracotta button | Take warmth from the subject's own material, a photograph, or a full warm field |
| High-contrast or italic serif display as the shortcut to "premium" | convergence | A face from the subject's world, whatever its class |
| One word of the headline in italic, colour or underline | convergence; no real difference in the study (4% against 7%) | One face, one colour; the words carry the emphasis |
| Broadsheet cosplay: hairlines everywhere, dense columns, small tracked labels | convergence | Structure from space, tone and scale |
| Performative labels: "Field notes", "Quietly trusted by" | convergence | Plain section names |

**Your own measured default.** Impeccable reports that Claude renders warm, bookish, family and child-facing subjects as cream grounds, serif display with italic accents and lamplight, even when nothing asked for them. Treat that first palette as already spent. Books are also cloth, thread, jackets and endpapers, which span every colour.

## Gallery fashion: house style, against the evidence

These are *more* common on top-scored sites than on weak ones. They are the first devices generators copy from galleries, so they now read as costume. This skill avoids them as house style. Say so if you follow this, and drop it if the user's taste differs.

| Default | Basis | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monospace labels, mono as a "technical" voice | house; top sites 44% against 24% | The text face; tabular figures for data. Mono stays right for code |
| Index numbering: 01 / 02 / 03, "Step 02" | house; top sites 38% against 21% | Order by position, size or words; a real numbered list when order matters |
| Hairline rules, ruled grids, 1px outlined boxes | house; no real difference (61% against 52%) | Space, a tone step, a filled shape |
| Design-tool chrome as ornament: crosshairs, crop marks, rulers | house | Remove |
| Agency micro-meta: local time, coordinates, version stamps | house | Remove |

## Structure

| Default | Basis | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The stock order: hero, logos, three features, testimonial, pricing, call to action | convergence | The order of the argument: one section per claim with its proof |
| Every section in the same layout | convergence | A different composition from its neighbours |
| Everything centred | evidence: centred heroes 52% of weak sites, 39% overall | Left-anchored, one centred moment |
| Nine nearly equal font sizes | convergence | Two or three sizes doing the visible work, far apart |
| A modal for a task that needs no interruption | convergence | Inline, or a page of its own |
| A distinctive first view, then stock blocks | convergence | Carry the idea into every part |
| Invented social proof: logo rows, star ratings, "10,000+ teams" | convergence | Real proof, or none |

## Slides and documents

These are house observations, not measured.

| Default | Basis | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title plus three bullets on every slide | house | One message per slide, set as a sentence, with what the audience must see |
| An icon in front of every bullet or heading | house | Words; an icon only where it is faster to read than the word |
| Gradient or stock-photo title slide | house | The subject's own picture, or type and one colour field |
| An "Agenda" slide that lists the headings | house | Start with the point |
| Bold on every other phrase | house | Bold only what a skimming reader must not miss |
| A horizontal rule between every section, emoji in headings | house | Space and a clear heading |
| Charts in rainbow colours, legends far from the data | house | One colour for the finding, grey for the rest, labels on the data |

## Copy

The genre's words read as generated in any medium: unlock, supercharge, seamless, elevate, effortless, cutting-edge, all-in-one; the correction "not just X, it's Y"; three-part headlines; "Get started" and "Learn more" as labels. Controls name their action. Errors name the problem and the way out.

## Why removal is not enough

A design with every default removed and nothing in its place is an empty page with good manners, and anyone could guess it from the category plus the list of things to avoid. The defaults were filling a gap where an idea should have been. Fill that gap first: the idea from the subject, real content, real pictures or committed type. Then check this list.
